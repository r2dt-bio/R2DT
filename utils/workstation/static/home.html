R2DT workstation

Visualise RNA secondary structure in consistent, recognisable layouts — privately on this machine, with a history you can reopen anytime.

How it works

  1. Start with just workstation (Docker required).
  2. Pick a mode above — each has its own job list and launch form.
  3. Upload inputs, run the job, and reopen results anytime from that mode’s dashboard.
  4. Cache lives in ~/.r2dt-workstation (mounted at /workspace in Docker).
